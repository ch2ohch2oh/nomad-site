03

With a job offer

Start with the job, your background and the employer’s supporting evidence. A job title alone does not establish eligibility.

General Employment Policy (GEP)

Professionals applying through the general employment route

An employer-backed route for a genuine professional vacancy. Your qualifications or experience must fit the role, and pay must match the local market.

Check GEP eligibility
Requirements & application steps for General Employment Policy (GEP)

Requirements to check

  • A confirmed Hong Kong offer for work not readily filled by the local workforce.
  • Normally a relevant degree; documented technical qualifications, abilities or experience may be accepted in special cases.
  • GEP generally excludes Chinese residents of the Mainland. Overseas PRC passport holders have specific residence rules. Check nationality exclusions too.

Application stages

  1. Agree the role and remuneration with the employer.
  2. Prepare personal credentials, the contract and employer supporting documents.
  3. Submit the online application and follow the approval and entry instructions.
Initial stay
Normally up to 36 months initially, or the contract length if shorter.
After arrival
Continue to meet GEP requirements. On employment condition, get approval before changing jobs; different rules apply to approved top-tier entrants.
Full official instructions

Mainland professionals (ASMTP)

Chinese residents of the Mainland with a professional job offer

The Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals is the corresponding employer-backed route for Mainland Chinese residents. Eligible graduates should also compare IANG.

Check ASMTP requirements
Requirements & application steps for Mainland professionals (ASMTP)

Requirements to check

  • A genuine vacancy and confirmed offer relevant to your qualifications or experience.
  • Skills not readily available locally and a market-level remuneration package.
  • Normally a relevant degree, with documented alternatives considered in special circumstances.

Application stages

  1. Confirm the appropriate scheme with the employer.
  2. Prepare the employment contract, qualifications and employer evidence.
  3. Apply online; after approval, complete the applicable Mainland exit and Hong Kong entry formalities.
Initial stay
Temporary residence on the conditions stated in the approval.
After arrival
Continued eligibility matters. Check approval requirements before changing employment.
Full official instructions

Technical Professionals Stream

Experienced non-degree professionals in specified skilled trades

A quota-based stream within GEP and ASMTP. It covers trades on the Technical Professional List, rather than every technical job.

See eligible trades & quota rules
Requirements & application steps for Technical Professionals Stream

Requirements to check

  • Age 18–40 at entry application.
  • Meet the listed trade’s qualifications, experience and registration or licensing requirements.
  • A confirmed offer meeting the trade’s minimum contract term and remuneration rules; quota must be available.

Application stages

  1. Match the actual job to the current Technical Professional List.
  2. Check the trade-specific evidence and quota with the employer.
  3. Submit the stream-specific online application.
Initial stay
Normally 36 months initially; certain applicants needing local registration initially receive 24 months.
After arrival
Employer changes require approval within the same trade. Trade changes are not permitted during the existing stay. Check licensing requirements for renewal.
Full official instructions
04

Talent & graduate routes

These routes can remove the need for an offer at entry, when you meet their specific rules. They still have conditions, expiry dates and renewal requirements.

Top Talent Pass Scheme (TTPS)

High earners and graduates of eligible universities

No Hong Kong job offer is needed at entry. Choose the category matching your income, university and experience.

Check TTPS categories & university list
Requirements & application steps for Top Talent Pass Scheme (TTPS)

Requirements to check

  • Check the category table below and the official definitions.
  • For B/C, verify the eligible university, qualifying bachelor’s degree and credential authentication rules.
  • Check nationality and residence restrictions. The initial stay is available only once.

Application stages

  1. Identify A, B or C and collect proof.
  2. Submit the online entry application.
  3. After approval, follow the e-Visa and entry instructions.
Initial stay
Normally 36 months for A; 24 months for B/C.
After arrival
Employment with stable income or an established/joined Hong Kong business is needed for extension.
Full official instructions

Which TTPS category fits?

Entry categories · no local job offer required
CategoryCore requirement
A · high incomeAt least HK$2.5 million qualifying income in the preceding year. Personal investment income is excluded.
B · experienced graduateEligible university bachelor’s degree, plus at least three years’ work experience in the past five years.
C · recent graduateEligible bachelor’s degree earned in the past five years, with less than three years’ experience. Annual quota applies; qualifying non-local Hong Kong undergraduate graduates are excluded.

The university list and degree rules matter. A university’s general reputation alone is insufficient.

Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS)

Skilled applicants qualifying through assessment and selection

No local job offer is generally required for entry. Meet the prerequisites, then qualify through the General Points Test or the exceptional-achievement route. Selection remains discretionary.

Read the QMAS scheme rules
Requirements & application steps for Quality Migrant Admission Scheme (QMAS)

Requirements to check

  • Age 18+, adequate financial resources, good character and normally a first degree or an accepted alternative.
  • For the General Points Test, meet the current threshold below. More criteria can strengthen consideration; passing does not guarantee approval.

Application stages

  1. Check prerequisites and choose one assessment route.
  2. Prepare evidence and apply online.
  3. Await selection and the application decision.
Initial stay
Normally 36 months under the general test, subject to the existing-talent-holder exception below; eight years under the achievement test.
After arrival
General-test entrants must demonstrate steps to settle locally; further extensions require settlement and contribution.
Full official instructions

Non-local graduates (IANG)

Eligible Hong Kong graduates and qualifying GBA campus graduates

Recent graduates applying within six months of graduation can apply without a job offer. Later applicants need a graduate-level offer with market-level remuneration.

Check IANG graduate requirements
Requirements & application steps for Non-local graduates (IANG)

Requirements to check

  • A qualifying full-time, locally accredited Hong Kong undergraduate degree or higher, or a qualifying Greater Bay Area campus qualification.
  • Use the graduation certificate date to determine the six-month window.
  • Nationality-specific restrictions apply. Check the GBA arrangement’s current scope and availability.

Application stages

  1. Confirm the qualification and recent/non-recent category.
  2. Prepare graduation evidence and, if needed, an offer.
  3. Submit the IANG application and follow entry or change-of-status instructions.
Initial stay
Normally 24 months initially; employment changes do not require prior approval during permitted stay.
After arrival
Check the current employment or business evidence required for extension.
Full official instructions

Entrepreneur & other routes

Building a business. The GEP entrepreneur route assesses economic contribution, a credible business plan, funding, local jobs and other factors. It generally excludes Mainland Chinese residents; overseas PRC passport holders have specific rules. Company registration alone does not secure residence.

Read the entrepreneur entry rules

Specialist routes also include TechTAS for qualifying technology employment, eligible second-generation Hong Kong families, working holidays and capital investment. Each has its own scope.

Renewals & permanent residence

Keep your permission current

Check your scheme’s extension window and evidence requirements early. An extension application awaiting a decision does not automatically let you remain beyond your current limit of stay.

Keep records of employment, business activity, residence and travel. TTPS and IANG allow employment changes during permitted stay without prior approval; employer-sponsored conditions require a separate check.

Employment & extension conditionsTTPS after arrivalEntry & stay arrangements

What the seven-year rule means

  1. Chinese citizens: one eligibility category requires at least seven continuous years of ordinary residence.
  2. Non-Chinese citizens: the seven continuous years must immediately precede the application; Hong Kong must also have been taken as the place of permanent residence. Application and approval are required.
  3. Residence is assessed: holding a visa for seven years alone is insufficient. Temporary absences do not automatically break ordinary residence; their reasons and your ongoing ties matter.

Lawful study, employment or residence may qualify as ordinary residence. Some categories are excluded, including foreign domestic-helper employment and government labour-importation schemes.

Costs & family

Budget for the whole move

  • Application and visa issuance fees
  • Credential verification and translations
  • Travel, housing deposit and living costs
  • Dependants’ applications and renewals

Plan around the stages

  1. Employer or personal evidence
  2. Assessment and any selection stage
  3. Approval, fee payment and entry

Use each scheme’s current processing guidance. Missing documents and selection can add time.

Specified schemes charge separate application and issuance fees, including for dependants. Check the current table before paying.

Bringing family

Eligible spouses or recognised partners and unmarried dependent children under 18 may qualify, subject to relationship, support and accommodation requirements. The recognised-partner rules are specific; cohabitation alone is insufficient.

Dependants of admitted professionals, eligible talent entrants and entrepreneurs generally may work. Student dependants face different rules. Each family member needs appropriate permission, normally linked to the sponsor’s stay.

Terms explained
ImmD
Hong Kong’s Immigration Department, which assesses entry and residence applications.
HKSAR
Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. It has its own immigration controls.
Employment condition
Your permission is tied to approved employment. Check approval requirements before changing work.
Time limitation only
Permission has an expiry date, even when it is not tied to one employer.
Ordinary residence
Lawful, voluntary residence for a settled purpose, assessed from your circumstances.
Right of abode
Permanent resident status with rights defined in Hong Kong law.
STEM
Science, technology, engineering and mathematics.
GBA campus
A qualifying Greater Bay Area institution under the specific IANG arrangements, not any university in the region.
